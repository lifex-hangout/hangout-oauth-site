About Hangout

Hangout - Date & Find Friends is a mobile app for meeting people, dating, and making friends. Members create a profile, connect with other people, and can report or block users. If you choose Google Sign-In, Hangout uses it to authenticate your account.

Visit the Hangout homepage to learn more about the app and how it handles your information.

Need Help?

If you have any questions, encounter a problem, or would like to report a bug, please contact us.

Contact

For support inquiries, please email

sysop@beesk.it

When contacting us, please include:

  • Your device model
  • Your operating system and version
  • Your Hangout app version
  • A description of the problem
  • Screenshots, if applicable

Report or Block a User

You can report or block users directly from within the Hangout app. If you encounter inappropriate behavior, harassment, spam, or other violations, please use the report feature in the app.

Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Hangout account and associated data, open your profile by tapping your profile picture in the top-left corner, then tap Settings in the top-right corner. Open FAQ and select “Request Account Deletion.”
